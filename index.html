DrMadina Junussova

I study how mining regions and towns in Central Asia can stay liveable through the energy transition: public policy, urban resilience and the futures of places built around a single industry.

Welcome

I am Urban Development Lead at the Graduate School of Development and Interim Director of the Civil Society Initiative at the University of Central Asia, where I am also a Senior Research Fellow at the Institute of Public Policy and Administration.

I lead UCA’s work on University Town Development and the Naryn Urban Resilience Programme, a five-year initiative to make Naryn a resilient mountain town through planning, training and coordination between agencies. My research covers just transition, decarbonisation, urban-rural linkages and the empowerment of youth and women.

Trained as an architect and urban planner in Almaty and as a policy scholar at Carleton University in Ottawa, I have drafted master plans for Kazakhstani cities, advised the OECD, UNECE and UNICEF, and taught civil servants from Afghanistan to Mongolia. I am the author of Cities and Local Governments in Central Asia (Routledge, 2020, open access).
